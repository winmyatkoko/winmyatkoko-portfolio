Context

Energy Tracker Asia (ETA) and Climate Impacts Tracker Asia (CITA) are two independent digital media publications focused on Asia's energy transition and climate impact reporting. When I joined the team at Cuttlefish Digital, both platforms were at early stages of their digital presence. ETA had an established website but limited audience infrastructure. CITA launched from scratch in mid-2023.

The brief was broad: grow both platforms into credible, high-reach publications that could influence the regional conversation on climate and clean energy. The constraint was real. This was a small, lean team with limited budget, operating across two separate publishing brands simultaneously.

My Role

As the lead digital growth strategist, I was responsible for the full owned media ecosystem: SEO strategy and execution, newsletter growth and engagement, paid distribution campaigns, audience segmentation, social media strategy, podcast promotion, publication distribution, push notification systems, and cross-channel analytics reporting. Working closely with my manager, I owned the growth strategy and analytics layer while coordinating with editorial and content teams on distribution and amplification.

Strategy & Execution

The approach evolved across three distinct phases, each responding to what the data was showing at the time.

In 2022, the priority was acquisition. For ETA's newsletter, I initiated a performance marketing program to grow subscriber volume, driving 10,362 net new subscribers in the first year. On SEO, we began building a systematic content and technical optimization program targeting regional energy and environmental keywords, establishing the domain authority foundation that would compound over subsequent years.

In 2023, the strategic focus shifted from volume to quality. I identified significant list degradation in the newsletter: inactive subscribers were inflating the total count and suppressing engagement rates. I implemented a full list hygiene and segmentation program, removing inactive contacts and rebuilding targeting around engaged audience cohorts. Open rates rose from 15% to 25%. Simultaneously, I launched CITA from zero, cross-acquiring initial audiences from ETA and building separate content distribution infrastructure for the new brand.

In 2024, the focus moved to deepening engagement and distribution efficiency. The most significant initiative was audience segmentation on paid channels. By April 2024, I replaced broad core audience targeting with precisely defined niche segments. For example, shifting from "all policymakers in Malaysia" to "policymakers with specific interests in renewable energy and clean tech." The impact was immediate and substantial: key conversion events (newsletter subscriptions, publication downloads, blog reads) increased by 1,116% compared to the pre-segmentation period. Average session duration increased by 427%.

Alongside paid strategy, I led a UX optimization initiative on ETA's blog pages that restructured the above-the-fold layout to bring article content into immediate view. This single change drove a 201.2% increase in engagement rate and a 30% decrease in bounce rate between Q1 and Q2 2024.

I also built and scaled new distribution channels across both platforms: iZooto push notifications (12,231 ETA subscribers, 4,076 CITA subscribers), WhatsApp broadcast channels, and LinkedIn Newsletter programs. Each channel was treated as a distinct audience relationship requiring its own content and engagement logic.

On the research side, I led an original analysis exploring the correlation between SEO ranking position and AI search referral traffic, testing whether better domain authority and organic ranking translated into higher visibility in AI-generated search results from platforms like ChatGPT and Perplexity. The findings informed a revised SEO and content strategy prioritizing high-authority content for pages most likely to benefit from AI referral growth.

Results

From 2022 to 2024, ETA's organic search traffic grew by 1,966% in user acquisition, 1,264% in page views, and over 2,000% in engaged sessions. CITA, launched in mid-2023, had already attracted 22,915 organic users by end of 2024 with strong upward momentum. Combined newsletter subscribers across both platforms reached over 14,000 active contacts. The podcast programs across ETA (30,000+ downloads, 28 episodes) and CITA (67,000+ downloads, 20 episodes) established both platforms as credible audio voices in the regional climate conversation.

Content from both platforms is now cited in ministry briefings, referenced in international policy discussions, and covered by major media outlets — outcomes that reflect the cumulative effect of sustained, strategic audience growth rather than any single campaign.

What I Learned

The most important insight from this project is that audience quality compounds over time in a way that audience volume does not. Every decision to prioritize engagement over raw numbers (list hygiene, niche segmentation, UX optimization) paid larger returns in subsequent periods than the equivalent investment in acquisition volume would have.

I also learned that in a lean team, strategic clarity about which channels to build deeply versus maintain lightly is more valuable than trying to optimize everything simultaneously. The wins came from full commitment to a smaller number of well-executed growth levers.
